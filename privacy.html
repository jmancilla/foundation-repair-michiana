Privacy Policy

How FOUNDATION WORKS collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Last updated: August 11, 2026

1. Overview

FOUNDATION WORKS ("we," "our," or "us") operates foundationrepairmichiana.com as a referral service that connects homeowners with licensed, independent foundation repair and waterproofing contractors in the Michiana region. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through our website, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website or submitting a free inspection request, you agree to the practices described below.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information you choose to provide. When you submit the Free Inspection & Quote Request form, we collect:

  • Your full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Nearest city (Michiana region)
  • Details about your property and the structural issues you report
  • Your preferred inspection date

We do not collect payment information, and we do not require an account to use our website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Process and respond to your inspection or quote request
  • Contact you by phone or email to schedule and confirm appointments
  • Connect you with licensed, independent contractors who can address your needs
  • Improve our services and website

4. Cookies and Analytics

Our website is a static site and does not place advertising or tracking cookies on your device. We may use standard server logs to understand general traffic patterns and diagnose technical issues. Should we add analytics tools in the future, we will update this policy.

5. How We Share Your Information

Your request details are shared only with the independent, licensed contractors we refer you to and who are selected to follow up on your inspection request. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

6. Data Retention

We retain lead information only as long as necessary to complete your request, provide ongoing service, or comply with legal obligations. You may ask us to delete your information at any time by contacting us using the details below.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of the personal information we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for adults who own or manage property. We do not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal information, contact us at:
